Know if a rental makes money before you call the agent.

A Chrome extension that shows cash flow, cap rate and cash-on-cash return right on property listings, in the US on Zillow and Redfin, and in Australia on Domain and realestate.com.au.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

How it works

Reads the listing

The price is filled in from the page you're looking at, plus the rent estimate and HOA fee where the site shows them. On Australian sites, enter rent per week and strata per quarter.

Uses your numbers

Set your down payment, interest rate, taxes, insurance, vacancy and repairs once. Every number can be edited.

Stays on your computer

The calculations run in your browser. Your listings and saved deals are never sent anywhere.
